# ReadAloud on iPhone Duo

Public visual materials for ReadAloud's App Store editorial feature nomination.

## Captured screen layouts

![ReadAloud on iPhone Duo: three simulator screen layouts](assets/readaloud-iphone-duo-overview.jpg)

The board shows real ReadAloud screens captured in the iPhone Duo simulator: outer-display portrait reading, inner-display landscape with Original and Reading side by side, and inner-display portrait reading.

The display outlines and background are illustrative. The app screens come from the supplied simulator captures and are only scaled to fit this overview; this composite is for editorial context, not an App Store product-page screenshot.

## What is visible

- Reading view with the current sentence highlighted and playback controls
- Original document beside the Reading view in landscape
- Library and reading screens across portrait and landscape captures

Further screenshots and a demo video can be added after the final review of those materials.
